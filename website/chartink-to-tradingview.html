How to Import a Chartink Screener into TradingView

By Alen Sarang Satheesh • Updated September 2026 • 4 min read

Chartink is where Indian traders run their daily technical scans; TradingView is where they analyze charts. The frustrating bottleneck has always been moving tickers between them: a scan can return 40, 80, or 150 stocks, and TradingView's free plan caps you at only 30 symbols in a single list.

Below, we examine both methods: the tedious manual copy-paste workaround, and the seamless 1-click extension method with instant daily re-sync.

Method 1: The Tedious Manual Way

Without an extension, transferring stocks from Chartink to TradingView requires painful repetitive work every morning:

  1. Run your screener on Chartink.com.
  2. Click the "Copy" or "CSV" export button at the bottom of the table.
  3. Open a spreadsheet or text editor to clean the column data, removing company names, percentages, and prices to isolate ticker symbols.
  4. Add NSE: or BSE: prefixes to each ticker so TradingView doesn't confuse Indian stocks with international symbols.
  5. Join the symbols with commas (e.g. NSE:RELIANCE, NSE:TCS, NSE:INFY).
  6. Open TradingView, click Import Watchlist, and paste the string.

The Major Drawbacks of Manual Import:

1) Free Plan Limitation: If your Chartink scan returns more than 30 stocks, TradingView truncates your list or prompts you to upgrade to Pro ($14.95/mo).
2) Zero Daily Sync: When market conditions change tomorrow, you must re-do the entire copy, format, and paste routine from scratch.

Method 2: The 1-Click Extension Method (Recommended)

With the free Unlimited Watchlists Chrome Extension, the entire workflow becomes automated.

Step 1: Install & Pin the Extension

Install the extension from the Chrome Web Store and pin the icon to your toolbar for quick access.

Step 2: Open Any Chartink Screener

Navigate to any screener on Chartink (such as "15 Min Volume Shockers", "52 Week High Breakouts", or "RSI Bullish Reversal").

The extension automatically detects the scan table and injects a dedicated "Import to Watchlist" button directly above the results table:

Step 3: Click "Import to Watchlist"

Click the button once. The extension reads every single page of results (even if there are 200+ stocks across 5 pagination pages), formats the tickers, and creates a brand-new watchlist named after your screener inside your TradingView side panel.

Step 4: The 1-Click Re-Sync Feature

Here is the game changer: imported Chartink watchlists permanently remember their original screener URL!

Next morning when markets open, you don't even need to open Chartink. Simply click the circular ↺ Re-Sync icon next to the watchlist name. The extension queries Chartink in the background and refreshes the list with today's live results in 1 second.

Pro Tips for TradingView Screener Workflows

  • Spacebar Chart Cycling: Once your scan is loaded, press Space on your keyboard. TradingView immediately switches to the next ticker without page reload, allowing you to review 50 charts in under 2 minutes.
  • Right-Click Color Flags & Notes: As you review setups, right-click any ticker to flag high-probability setups with Green, Blue, Gold, or Purple dots, or write trade notes like "Buy > 380 SL 365".
  • Multi-Screener Structure: Import multiple scans (e.g. one for Intraday Breakouts, one for Weekly Swings). Each scan stays neatly organized in its own tab.
