★

Master Full Keyboard Shortcut Navigation

While viewing your TradingView chart or browsing the sidepanel, use instant shortcuts without touching your mouse:

  • Space or ↓ Down Arrow: Jump to next ticker and open chart.
  • Shift + Space or ↑ Up Arrow: Jump back to previous ticker and open chart.
  • → Right Arrow: Switch to next watchlist seamlessly (chart remains untouched).
  • ← Left Arrow: Switch to previous watchlist seamlessly (chart remains untouched).
  • Enter: Open the chart for the currently highlighted stock on demand.
  • Alt + W: Instantly open or close the sidepanel dock on TradingView.
