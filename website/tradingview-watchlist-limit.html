TradingView Watchlist Limit: Pricing, Constraints & Free Solution

By Alen Sarang Satheesh • Updated September 2026 • 6 min read

TradingView is undeniably the world's most popular charting platform, but its free tier comes with artificial constraints that severely handicap active traders: you are permitted exactly one watchlist, and that single list is hard-capped at 30 symbols.

If you follow multiple sectors, swing setups, and daily breakout scans, 30 tickers is simply not enough. In this guide, we analyze the official TradingView tier limits, the cost to unlock them, and how to get truly unlimited watchlists for free.

Official TradingView Tier Watchlist Limits

TradingView reserves multi-watchlist management for its paid subscription plans:

  • Free Basic Plan: Exactly 1 watchlist, maximum 30 stocks. No color markers, no sub-tabs.
  • Essential Plan ($14.95 / month): Up to 5 watchlists, maximum 1,000 stocks per list. Cost: ~$180 / year.
  • Plus Plan ($29.95 / month): Up to 10 watchlists, maximum 1,000 stocks per list. Cost: ~$360 / year.
  • Premium Plan ($59.95 / month): Unlimited watchlists, maximum 1,000 stocks per list. Cost: ~$720 / year.

How the Extension Bypasses the Limit

TradingView enforces its watchlist limits on its cloud servers: when a free user creates a second watchlist or adds a 31st symbol, TradingView's API rejects the request and presents a paywall modal.

The Unlimited Watchlists Chrome Extension bypasses this entirely by managing watchlists in the Chrome Side Panel API and injecting a dedicated dock directly into TradingView's right toolbar.
